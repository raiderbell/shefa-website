## Core principle

BLUF on every page — lead with the conclusion. Then support it. Never bury the point.

## Hard no list

Never use these words or phrases in Shefa copy:
- "visionary" / "innovative" / "thought leader" / "passionate"
- "game-changing" / "revolutionary" / "transformative"
- "kindly" (as in "kindly reach out")
- Emojis in professional communication
- Fake-humble anecdotes ("I was sitting in an airport...")

## Tone calibration

- Register: professional with a pulse. Warm but not casual.
- Warmth scale: brief warm opening, then direct.
- Never condescending. Never passive-aggressive. Never perform humility.
- Mission tone stays sincere — never sand the purpose out of the voice.

## Structure rules

- Lead with the point, then illustrate
- Build for skimmers: headers as navigation
- Short as possible, clear as needed
- Close every page with a clear next step or open door

## What "premium" means here

Premium through specificity, not adjectives. Show rigor. Name criteria. Describe process.
Premium without bragging — the work speaks, not the descriptor.

## Litmus test

Would a premium client, an investor, and a future ecosystem partner all feel they're in the right place?
If not, revise.
